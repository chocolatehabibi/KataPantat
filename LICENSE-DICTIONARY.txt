KataPantat Dictionary Data

KataPantat uses vocabulary data derived from the Malay-language
edition of Wiktionary through Kaikki.org / Wiktextract.

Source:
https://kaikki.org/mswiktionary/

The Kaikki machine-readable dictionary data is extracted from
Wiktionary and is made available under the same licenses as
Wiktionary, including Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
(CC BY-SA) and the GNU Free Documentation License (GFDL).

The source data is updated regularly.

KataPantat's dictionary processing extracts five-letter
alphabetic entries for use in the game.

The extracted data may contain uncommon, technical, archaic,
variant, or otherwise unsuitable entries. KataPantat therefore
uses a separate curated answer list for daily puzzles.

KataPantat does not reproduce the proprietary contents of
Kamus Dewan or other proprietary dictionary databases.
